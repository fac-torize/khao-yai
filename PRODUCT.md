# Khao Yai trip
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Thai-speaking traveler planning October 8–10, staying at atta Lakeside Resort.

## Product Purpose
A usable travel itinerary with restaurants, cafés, actual venue photos, maps, weather, and direct references.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve all eleven requested venues. Never fabricate facts, imagery, coordinates, opening hours, or forecasts. Label proposed itinerary times separately from sourced facts and user-provided travel times. Existing Next.js project. Year 2026 and private-car travel are disclosed working assumptions; origin, group size, and pace remain open after an optional clarification request.

## Evidence on Hand
Official Kirimaya, Safari Matcha and Toscana Valley pages; Wongnai venue listings; Ryoii, Soithong and Just Roam Around venue articles. Saalow has a reviewer source with an explicit image reuse prohibition; link to that gallery instead of copying its photos.

## Product Principles
- Facts have sources.
- Uncertainty stays visible.
- Navigation should be convenient on mobile.
- Leave time to enjoy the accommodation.
